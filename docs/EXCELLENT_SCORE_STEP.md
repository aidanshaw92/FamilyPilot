# Excellent: the score step (for review; not for merge without your go)

2026-10-08. This is the optional part of `EXCELLENT_RULE_V2.md` §1.5. It's the only part of the activity-evidence rule
that moves rankings. It is stacked on the R4 implementation (`EXCELLENT_ACTIVITY_EVIDENCE.md`), which leaves rankings
untouched.

## The change

The age factor of the Family Fit score carries 25% of its weight. Today it is either:

- 96, when the venue's own recommended range covers the children; or
- 75 (unknown), when there is no range.

With this step, for a venue with no recommended range, a reviewed **permanent provision** for a child's age sets it to:

| The provision covers | Age factor |
| --- | ---: |
| every child of 12 months or more | 88 |
| some of them | 80 |
| none, or a household of babies only | 75 (unchanged) |

A programme on set days never counts, and a stated recommended range always decides first.

## What to weigh

- **The case for it:** the venues that move up say, in their own words, that they have something permanent for these
  children's ages.
- **The case against:** rankings change for every household with a child of a year or more. 11 venues gain, and venues
  with no reviewed evidence (yet) lose ground for reasons that have nothing to do with them. The reviewed set is small
  (14 items), so the ranking would partly reflect which pages a person has read so far.
