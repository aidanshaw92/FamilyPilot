### 1. Natural History Museum: closed Friday 9 October 2026 and 24–26 December
- **Source:** https://www.nhm.ac.uk/visit.html (read 8 Oct)
- **Wording:** "Closed 9 October and 24–26 December … Our South Kensington site will be closed on Friday 9 October 2026, as we're hosting a charity gala."
- **Interpretation:** the whole South Kensington site (not Tring) is shut on 9 Oct 2026 and on 24, 25, 26 Dec. The homepage's "Open every day" is standing text that lists only 24–26 Dec; it does not say the museum is open on 9 Oct.
- **Effect:** a plan for those dates is refused ("The South Kensington museum is closed on Friday 9 October 2026 for a charity gala"), and Home and Explore say Closed today on the day. Other dates are untouched. 9 October is today, so from tomorrow only the Christmas closure matters.
- **If Unknown:** the app uses Google's hours and will plan a day to a shut museum on those dates.

### 2. Science Museum: closed 24–26 December; open daily 10:00–18:00
- **Source:** https://www.sciencemuseum.org.uk/visit (read 8 Oct)
- **Wording:** "The museum is open daily from 10.00–18.00 (except for 24–26 December when the museum is closed)."
- **Interpretation:** whole museum; closed 24, 25, 26 Dec; otherwise 10:00–18:00 daily. (Google agrees on the weekly pattern.)
- **Effect:** those three dates are refused; a visit running past 18:00 is refused.
- **If Unknown:** a Christmas-week plan is built for a closed museum.

### 3. Science Museum: buggies allowed; bulky ones may go to a buggy park in some areas
- **Source:** https://www.sciencemuseum.org.uk/visit/young-explorers-guide-science-museum (read 8 Oct)
- **Wording:** "Buggies are allowed in the museum and galleries. However, in certain areas, you may be asked to leave your pram in a buggy park as it will be too bulky to be allowed into the area or exhibit. Buggy parking is available free of charge in the Spare Room, located opposite The Garden gallery on level -1. Please ensure that the pushchairs are empty."
- **Interpretation:** buggies are welcome; some areas need them parked; free parking on level -1. The page does not say which areas.
- **Effect:** nobody is refused. A household bringing a buggy sees a "Check before you go" note naming the buggy park.
- **If Unknown:** that note is hidden; nothing else changes.

### 4. Discover Children's Story Centre: festive closure and hours
- **Source:** https://discover.org.uk/your-visit/ (read 8 Oct)
- **Wording:** "Discover is open every day, 10am - 5pm … We're closed on 24, 25, 26, 31 December and 1 January over the festive period."
- **Interpretation:** the whole centre; closed 24, 25, 26 Dec 2026 and 31 Dec 2026, 1 Jan 2027 (the page gives no year; the next occurrence is the only reading possible); otherwise 10:00–17:00 daily.
- **Effect:** those five dates are refused; a visit running past 17:00 is refused.
- **If Unknown:** a New Year's Eve plan is built for a closed centre.

### 5. Discover: no buggies or pushchairs in storytelling or play areas
- **Source:** https://discover.org.uk/getting-here/ (read 8 Oct)
- **Wording:** "There is buggy parking available on our Ground Floor. We do not allow buggies and pushchairs in any of our storytelling or play areas. If you have a smaller baby we recommend bringing a sling."
- **Interpretation:** the storytelling and play areas (the paid Story Worlds, which are what the ticket is for) are buggy-free; the café and bookshop are open to everyone.
- **Effect:** *as a warning only (proposed):* a household that brings a buggy gets a prominent warning in the venue's words plus the card-6 note, and still gets a plan. *As a restriction on the core visit:* a household that says buggy access is a must-have is told Discover does not fit them (no plan). I propose the warning because of card 6.
- **If Unknown:** no warning at all; a family with a pram hears nothing until they arrive.

### 6. Discover: the venue's own exception (decide together with 5)
- **Source:** https://discover.org.uk/your-visit/faqs/ (read 8 Oct)
- **Wording:** "If a pushchair is needed (twins, a sleeping child), ask front of house; they will advise and accommodate where needed."
- **Interpretation:** the rule in card 5 has an exception the venue itself states: ask, and they will accommodate.
- **Effect:** quoted next to the card-5 warning, so a family is told both. If you chose "restriction" for card 5, this still travels with the refusal message.
- **If Unknown:** card 5 would be shown without the venue's own softening, harsher than the venue is.

### 7. London Zoo: hours change on 24 October
- **Source:** https://www.londonzoo.org/plan-your-visit/opening-times (read 8 Oct)
- **Wording:** "Dates Opening Last Entry Closing — 3 September - 23 October 2026 10am 4pm 5pm — 24 October 2026 - 12 February 2027 10am 3pm 4pm"
- **Interpretation:** 10:00–17:00 until 23 Oct; 10:00–16:00 (last entry 15:00) from 24 Oct to 12 Feb 2027. **Google shows 10am–5pm every day, which is wrong from 24 October.**
- **Effect:** from 24 Oct a visit that would run past 16:00 is refused (e.g. 1pm–4:30pm on 25 Oct); a 10:30am visit for two and a half hours is built and says "Google lists 10am to 5pm; the venue's website says 10am to 4pm. We've used the venue's website."
- **If Unknown:** plans from 24 Oct can run an hour past closing.

### 8. Mudchute Park and Farm: the farm is open every day
- **Source:** https://www.mudchute.org/plan-your-visit/opening-times (read 8 Oct)
- **Wording:** "Farm: Every day 9am–4pm (animals go in from 3pm) Park: Every day dawn–dusk"
- **Interpretation:** the farm is open 9am–4pm, seven days a week. **Google shows Mondays as closed.** (Only the courtyard and pets corner close on Mondays; that is a separate rule and only warns.)
- **Effect:** a Monday is no longer refused or labelled Closed today; the plan says Google and the venue differ and which was used.
- **If Unknown:** every Monday plan is refused although the farm is open.

### 9. Gunnersbury Park: park and museum hours
- **Source:** https://www.visitgunnersbury.org/plan-your-visit (read 8 Oct)
- **Wording:** "Opening Hours Museum Tuesday-Sunday 10am to 4:30pm Park Daily between 7am until dusk"
- **Interpretation:** the park is open 7am until dusk; the museum Tuesday to Sunday 10am–4:30pm (closed Mondays). **Google shows the park open 24 hours.**
- **Effect:** the plan tells the family the sources disagree. No clock time is invented for "dusk", so planning keeps Google's schedule and carries the note. The museum's Monday closure only warns (it is an area, not the whole venue).
- **If Unknown:** an early-morning or late-evening plan is accepted with no note.

## What this wave does and does not do for the ten venues

**Wave 1 is about safety, not readiness.** It stops the app sending a family to a closed place or running past closing, and warns about buggies. **It does not by itself make any venue newly "recommendation-ready"** (my internal rating: opening hours, cost, a way there, something for children, a toilet). Today only the Royal Air Force Museum meets it.

The app does not hide venues below that rating; they appear with gaps named as "to check". The rating shows what a *genuinely useful* beta needs on top of wave 1. **Correction (second one):** I earlier said 6 of 10. That counted Mudchute's playground card, whose value is "adjacent" (a playground next door), which publishes nothing.
