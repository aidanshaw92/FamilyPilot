# Reviewer guide: checking what a venue's own website says

For anyone asked to review FamilyPilot's venue facts. You need no knowledge of the app or of code. It takes about 2 minutes to read; the same text is at the top of the review page.

## What this is for

FamilyPilot helps parents in London pick a day out. For each venue it wants to be able to say things like "there are baby-changing facilities", "closed on 9 October" or "step-free access to all galleries". A computer found these statements on the venue's own website. Before any is shown to a parent, a **person** checks it. That is you.

A wrong "yes" sends a family on a wasted journey with a tired toddler. A wrong "closed" or "not accessible" hides a good place from a family who needed it. Your job is to stop both.

## What you see on each card

- **The venue and the kind of fact** (for example, *Mudchute Park and Farm · toilets*).
- **Proposed:** the sentence FamilyPilot would like to use. Words in **yellow** are not in the venue's quote at all. Read those first.
- **The grey quote:** the venue's own words, shown in the words around it on its page, with a link to open the whole page and the date it was read.
- **Chips** such as "an independent check doubted it" or "a model reading flagged it". These are hints about where to look. They are not evidence, and the absence of a hint does not mean a card is fine.

## The four buttons

| Button | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | The venue's words say exactly this, for this site, with the same conditions. |
| **Edit meaning** | The quote supports something narrower or different. Write what it does support. |
| **Reject** | The statement is wrong, or says more than the quote. |
| **Mark unknown** | The page does not settle it, is out of date, or is about somewhere else. |
| Undo last | Takes back your previous decision. |

## Five checks, every card

1. **Right place?** Is the quote about *this* site? Not another branch, a nearby car park, a sister venue or a partner.
2. **Exactly this?** Every number, day, time, age, and words like "all", "every", "free", "no", "always".
3. **Any condition dropped?** "Weekdays only", "temporarily", "until June", "members", "booked in advance", "in the café only".
4. **Same yes or no?** If a "not" or "no" is highlighted, or the quote has one the proposal lacks, the meaning may have flipped.
5. **Still current?** The date read is on the card. A closure notice for a past date, or "until July 2025", is over.

## If you are not sure

**Choose Mark unknown, not Approve.** "Unknown" costs nothing: the app simply does not say it. Use only what the page says. Do not use your own memory of the place, what you would expect, or an AI tool's opinion; none of those is evidence for a parent. If the words around the quote are not enough, open the page.

## Take extra care with

- **Prices and free entry.** Free for whom? Which days? For a ticket or just the park?
- **Opening hours and closures.** A whole-site closure is serious. Is it the whole site or one gallery? Which dates exactly?
- **"Not accessible", "no step-free access", pushchair or buggy rules.** These can hide a venue from a family. Does the rule apply to the whole visit or one area?
- **Which ages a place is for.** A programme on certain days is not the same as a place being suitable for that age.
- **Parking.** General parking, Blue Badge bays and "parking nearby" are three different things.

## Four short examples (illustrative)

- **Approve.** Proposed: *"Toilets, including a disabled toilet, are available in the courtyard."* Quote: *"Toilets, including a disabled toilet, are available in the courtyard…"* Nothing added, nothing missing, right site. Approve.
- **Edit.** Proposed: *"Accessible toilet on the ground floor, with no RADAR key needed."* Quote: *"There is an accessible toilet in the museum."* The floor and the key are not in the quote. Edit to: *"There is an accessible toilet in the museum."*
- **Reject.** Proposed: *"The 4D Theatre is closed until 31 December 2026."* Quote: a listing of events whose pages all end on 31 December. A date printed on every event is not a closure. Reject.
- **Unknown.** Proposed: *"No step-free access at the nearest station."* Quote: a notice that says *"until July 2025"*. That date has passed, so the page cannot tell us today's position. Mark unknown.

## When you finish

Press **Export decisions** and send the downloaded file (`review-decisions.jsonl`) back. Your decisions are saved in your browser as you go, so you can stop and come back on the same computer. The page records how long each card is open; finish the card you are on before a break. There is no pass mark for speed. Accuracy comes first.

If a card confused you, say which (the venue and the kind of fact are shown) and in one sentence why. A confusing card is a fault in the card.
