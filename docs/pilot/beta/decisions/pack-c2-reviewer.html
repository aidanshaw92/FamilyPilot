How to review (read this first, 2 minutes)

What you are doing. Each card is one statement about a venue that FamilyPilot would show to parents. Decide whether the venue's own words (the grey quote, in the words around it on its page) support the statement exactly as written, for this venue, with the same conditions. You are not judging whether the venue is good, and you do not need to know anything about the app.

The buttons. Approve: the venue's words say exactly this and nothing less or more. Edit meaning: the quote supports something narrower or different; write what it does support. Reject: the statement is wrong, or says more than the quote. Mark unknown: the page does not settle it, it is unclear, out of date, or about somewhere else. Undo last takes back your previous decision.

Five checks on every card. (1) Is the quote about this site, not another branch, a nearby car park, or a partner? (2) Does it say exactly what is proposed: every number, day, age, "all", "free", "no"? (3) Is a condition missing ("weekdays only", "temporarily", "until June", "members")? (4) Does the proposal keep the quote's yes or no? Yellow words are not in the quote: read them first. A highlighted "not" or "no" is a possible flipped meaning. (5) Is it current? The date read is on the card; a closure or notice may be over.

If you are unsure, choose Mark unknown, not Approve. A wrong "yes" sends a family on a wasted journey; "unknown" costs nothing. Use only what the page says: not your memory, not what you would expect, and not an AI tool's opinion. If the surrounding text is not enough, open the page link.

Take extra care with prices and free entry; opening hours and closures; anything saying a place is not accessible, or has a pushchair or buggy rule; and statements about which ages a place is for.

Grouped cards. Where several equivalent cards are grouped, read the ones shown and approve them; an "Approve all N equivalent" button then unlocks. If you would reject even one, do not use it.

Saving. Your decisions are saved in this browser as you go. When finished (or to stop for the day) press Export decisions and send the downloaded file back. The page records how long each card is open, so finish the card you are on before a break. Take as long as you need: accuracy matters more than speed.
