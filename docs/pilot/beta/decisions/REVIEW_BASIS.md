# Review basis for the five-venue evidence: founder-reviewed, not independent

9 October 2026.

**The owner (founder) decided Pack A (6 cards), Pack B (6 cards) and Pack C (9 cards plus the 4 planted quality controls) personally. No one else read any card. This is a founder-reviewed batch. It is not independent verification.**

What that does and does not give:
- It gives a named human decision on every published card, with the venue's own quotation, source page and reading date, an edited wording where the owner changed it, and a tamper-evident audit trail (`audit-packAB.jsonl`, and the Pack C chain once returned).
- Pack C's four planted errors still measure whether the reading catches mistakes (bar: 3 of 4). They are a check on the reviewer's attention, not a second opinion.
- The process normally asks a second reader to re-check every edit and one in five approvals. Here the second reader is the same person, so that sample is listed in the manifest for completeness but is **not independent**. Anyone who later wants an independent check can use that list.
- The owner's wording edits are the owner's. The source check (`SOURCE_CHECK_AB.txt`) is mechanical and independent of the reviewer: it confirms the quoted words appear on the stored official page.

This basis is written into the manifest (`MANIFEST.md`, `claims/manifest.json`) and the header of the claims SQL.
