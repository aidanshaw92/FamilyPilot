## The family journey, by step, on the evidence that exists

| Step | Today (production claims) | After the reviewed facts |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | Works for all ten | Same |
| Understand why it suits | Reasons from confirmed facts; unconfirmed things are named, not hidden | Adds confirmed activity ages, so reasons name each child |
| See restrictions | **Not reliably.** A plan to the Natural History Museum on a closed date, or a buggy to Discover's play areas, is built with nothing flagged | Closures refuse the date; a pushchair restriction refuses a buggy-must-have household and warns one that brings a buggy |
| Compare | Home / Explore order unchanged | Unchanged (Fit v2 stays off) |
| Plan, save, reopen | Works; the saved plan keeps its "check before you go" lines | Same, with the new lines |
| Confirmed vs needs checking | Per-field labels, three access states, unknown never reads as incompatible | Same |

**The gap that matters for inviting families:** "See restrictions" does not work until a person has decided the closures, restrictions and hours. That is the review (96 decisions; 31 expert, 5 of them yours). Until then production plans can send a family to a closed museum without a flag. This is why review is on the critical path, not a nice-to-have.

## What I would not publish without a person (unchanged)

High-impact closures, accessibility restrictions, and age claims. Two models agreeing is not approval.
