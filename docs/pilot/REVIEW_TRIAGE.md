## Modelled workload

Reading every proposed item one at a time: **30.4 minutes**. With triage (individual items and spot-checks read in full, batch groups by sample, rejections glanced at): **32.6 minutes**. Source pages to open: 34 for every proposed item, 35 for the targeted queue.

Triage does not make this queue faster, and it was not built to: 75 of the 99 proposed items are high-impact by design (prices, accessibility, negatives, ages, rules and conflicts are where a wrong "yes" costs a family a wasted journey), and 10 are low-impact and clean. What it adds is control: every automatic acceptance has a chance of being read, every item an independent check doubts is routed to a person with the reason, and unsupported proposals are stopped before anyone reads them. The honest answer to "how long will 50 venues take" is: unknown until a person has worked a real session with the timers on.
