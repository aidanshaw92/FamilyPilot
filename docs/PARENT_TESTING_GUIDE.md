# FamilyPilot — Parent Testing Guide

**Version:** Early testing build (Phase 2 remediation)  
**URL:** https://family-pilot-seven.vercel.app/  
**Duration:** 5–10 minutes per tester

---

## Before you start

FamilyPilot is an early testing version. Some venues, prices, and availability information are prototype data — treat recommendations as examples, not live facts.

Works best on a phone browser (Safari or Chrome). Add to Home Screen for an app-like experience.

---

## Tester instructions

1. Open the link on your phone.
2. Spend **5–10 minutes** exploring naturally.
3. Imagine you are deciding **where to take your family this weekend**.
4. Try these actions:
   - Read **Today's Pick** on Home
   - Tap **Explore** and change a filter
   - **Save** a place (heart icon) and check **Saved**
   - Open a **venue** detail page
   - Try **Need something now** (emergency shopping flow)
   - Open one utility: **Packing**, **Car fit**, or **Plan a holiday**
5. When finished, tap **Profile → Send feedback** (or use the questions below).

---

## Feedback questions

Please answer in your own words:

1. What did you think the app was for?
2. Was that clear within the first 10 seconds?
3. Which feature felt most useful?
4. Which part was confusing?
5. Did you trust the recommendations?
6. What information did you expect but could not find?
7. Would you use this to decide where to go at the weekend?
8. Would you use Need Now while away from home?
9. Which feature would make you return?
10. Would you recommend it to another parent?
11. What is the first thing you would remove?
12. What is the first thing you would add?

---

## What we are not testing yet

- Live maps or navigation
- Real shop inventory
- Booking or payments
- Account sign-in
- Push notifications

Focus on clarity, trust, and whether the app helps you decide — not on data accuracy.
