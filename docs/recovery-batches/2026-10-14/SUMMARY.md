## How each batch-2 place earned its place

A score from: a Wikidata item (CC0) or an OS Open Greenspace polygon (OGL) that agrees on the name and place (2 each); the venue's own website in the source (1) and, where its page was fetched once and names the venue, 1.5 more; a Wikipedia article (1); a mapped playground inside a park (1); a park of 3 hectares or more (1); opening hours or a fee stated (0.5). For activities, farms, zoos and attractions, where those sources hardly reach, the venue's own page, fetched and checked, is the independent source. The batch stops when the evidence runs out; it is never padded to a target. Quality gates have already held back clubs, schools, shops, places of worship, commercial galleries and memorials (`docs/OPEN_DATA_FIRST_EXTRACT.md`).

## What this does not say

An independent identity is a name, a category and a position from a source with a licence. It is **not** a claim that the place has toilets, opens at any hour, is open today or suits a child of any age. Those stay "not yet reviewed" until our own evidence says otherwise.
