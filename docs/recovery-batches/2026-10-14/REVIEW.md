## What this does **not** say

An independent identity is a name, a category and a position from a source with a licence. It is **not** a statement that a place has toilets, a café, step-free access, opening hours, or suits a baby, a toddler or a ten-year-old. Those stay "not yet reviewed" until our own evidence says otherwise. OS describes its greenspaces as "likely to be accessible to the public" and generalises them automatically; that caveat applies to every park here.

## How each record was checked (automated)

- **Identity.** The name and position are the source's own (OS, Wikidata or OpenStreetMap), with a link to the record. No name, address or position from Google is used or written; a stored venue is read only to compute match numbers.
- **Continuing a venue we hold.** Name overlap, whether the stored position lies inside the park's polygon or within 150 m of the point, and category agreement. A match is "confident" only above fixed thresholds; everything else is in the exceptions.
- **Gates.** Held back, with a reason, in `A2-held.csv`: parks under 3 ha without a mapped play space, clubs, schools, burial grounds, allotments and golf courses; generic names; museums and attractions with no website of their own, or too few Wikipedia articles to show they are a destination; prehistoric monuments and arts venues that Wikidata files under "museum".
- **Evidence points,** each one checkable at the link: OS "named public park" polygon, its size, a mapped play space inside it, a Wikidata item, the number of Wikipedia articles, and the venue's own website, **fetched once** with robots.txt honoured, to see whether it answers and names the venue.
- **Duplicates.** Within 150 m of a place we already hold with a similar name, or within 120 m of a better-evidenced place in the same tranche: left out and listed. A place proposed in tranche A is dropped from tranche B.
- **Classification.** A castle, palace, abbey, house or dungeon that Wikidata files under "museum" is classed as an attraction, and the reason is shown on the record. Nothing is classed from a guess about the activity.
- **Shape.** The registry's own validator checks every entry (licence matches source, position inside Greater London, https website, no unknown key).
