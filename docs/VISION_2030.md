## 2030 north star

FamilyPilot is the **default family decision layer** — the first app parents open when they need to decide anything for their family, with enough confidence to act without opening Google, Maps, or five other apps.

One family profile. Every decision. Explainable confidence.

---

## What success looks like in 2030

| Signal | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Habit** | Parents open FamilyPilot before searching elsewhere |
| **Trust** | Recommendations feel personalised and honest |
| **Coverage** | Outings, food, travel, essentials, accessibility, SEND, social meetups, products |
| **Explainability** | Every suggestion answers "why this, for us, now?" |
| **Calm** | The app never feels noisy, salesy, or overwhelming |
| **Inclusive** | Accessibility and SEND are core, not add-ons |

## Product pillars (2030)

### 1. Discover — the world filtered for your family

Parents find parks, attractions, walks, museums, beaches, seasonal events, SEND-friendly and accessible venues — all ranked by Family Match, not popularity alone.

**Scope label:** Future Vision (Discover depth) · Long-term Research (seasonal events automation)

### 2. Eat — restaurants as first-class decisions

Every activity recommendation includes **Eat Nearby**. Restaurants are scored for family suitability, not review count.

**Scope label:** Post-MVP (Phase 3)

### 3. Together — meet other families fairly

One-phone meetups evolve into **Connected Families** — grandparents, friends, cousins — with combined Family Match and travel fairness.

**Scope label:** Post-MVP (Phase 6) · Future Vision (Phase 8 connected)

### 4. Plan — days and trips without a planner identity

Plan Your Day and Trips assemble editable itineraries. FamilyPilot remains a **decision platform**, not a calendar app.

**Scope label:** Post-MVP (Phase 7)

### 5. Move — car, packing, holidays

Car Fit, Packing, and Holiday comparison extend the same profile. Eventually: car recommendations, smart packing, full trip economics.

**Scope label:** MVP (partial — Car Fit, Packing, Holiday exist as prototypes) · Future Vision (car recommendations)

### 6. Now — urgent family essentials

Need Something Now expands to live stock where providers allow, with honest availability language.

**Scope label:** MVP (basic) · Future Vision (live inventory)

### 7. Include — accessibility and SEND by default

Every venue model supports accessibility facts and SEND attributes. Profile preferences hard-filter or warn when requirements aren't met.

**Scope label:** Post-MVP (Phases 4–5)

### 8. Save — time and money

Family Deals surface membership savings, verified kids-eat-free offers, and attraction discounts tied to the family's memberships.

**Scope label:** Long-term Research

### 9. Understand — Family DNA™

Over time, the profile learns patterns: preferred outing length, noise tolerance, favourite cuisines, typical budget — always transparent and editable.

**Scope label:** Long-term Research

## Experiences we explicitly will not become

| Anti-vision | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Social network with feeds | Conflicts with calm, decision-focused mission |
| Booking marketplace | We build confidence; partners handle transactions |
| Parenting content hub | Not our job to educate generically |
| Generic AI assistant | Intelligence stays invisible in scores and plans |
| Child-tracking or surveillance | Privacy violation |

---

## Technology enablers (2030, not commitments)

- Unified family profile with progressive enrichment
- Provider-agnostic places, weather, maps, inventory APIs
- Edge-computed Family Match with explainability JSON
- Shareable plan pages (no account required to read)
- Verified accessibility/SEND data partnerships
- Optional on-device preferences for sensitive flags

## Measuring progress

**Primary:** Do parents decide faster with more confidence?  
**Secondary:** Return usage, save rate, plan completion, tester NPS, trust ratings.

Not: raw DAU from content feeds, booking commission, or feature count.
